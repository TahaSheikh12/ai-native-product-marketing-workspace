# Automation and improvement backlog

## Status definitions

- **Idea:** worth investigating.
- **Validated:** repeated need and expected value are confirmed.
- **Approved:** owner has authorised the change and any access required.
- **Built:** solution exists but has not completed acceptance testing.
- **Adopted:** in regular use with a named owner.
- **Retired:** no longer useful or safe.

## Prioritisation questions

- Is the task frequent, repetitive and rules-based?
- Does automation preserve necessary human judgment?
- Are the inputs reliable and permitted for use?
- Is the output easy to review before it causes an external action?
- Who will maintain it when tools, data or processes change?
