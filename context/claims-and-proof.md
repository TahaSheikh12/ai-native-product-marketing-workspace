# Claims and proof register

Use this file to govern external-facing product and market claims. Only reviewed claims may be marked approved.

## Claim status

- **Proposed:** being developed; not approved for external use.
- **Approved:** reviewed by the appropriate owner for the stated audience and channel.
- **Restricted:** approved only with stated qualifier, audience or channel.
- **Rejected:** unsupported, misleading or no longer valid.
- **Expired:** approval or underlying evidence needs review.

## Rules

- Do not convert a directional finding into an absolute claim.
- Do not strengthen a claim while adapting it to another channel.
- Preserve qualifiers, population, time period and comparison basis.
- If evidence changes, review every linked claim.
- Keep prohibited or rejected claims visible so they are not accidentally recreated.
