# Two-minute tour

## If you are evaluating the thinking

Read these files in order:

1. [`../WORKSPACE.md`](../WORKSPACE.md) for the operating principles.
2. [`../workflows/README.md`](../workflows/README.md) for the MECE PMM lifecycle.

4. [`../context/evidence-register.md`](../context/evidence-register.md) and [`../context/claims-and-proof.md`](../context/claims-and-proof.md) for governance.
5. [`origin-and-evolution.md`](origin-and-evolution.md) for the real adoption and migration story.

## What to look for

- Whether decisions are separated from output formats.
- Whether analytical methods have clear units and exclusions.
- Whether the system prevents evidence from becoming an unsupported claim.
- Whether the structure can survive a change of AI platform.
- Whether the workspace remains usable by someone other than its creator.

## What this is not

- A software engineering portfolio.
- A collection of generic prompts.
- A claim that AI replaces PMM judgment.
- A repository of employer or customer work.

It is a practical demonstration of turning AI fluency into a repeatable product marketing operating system.
