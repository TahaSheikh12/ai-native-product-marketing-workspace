# PMM starter prompts

These prompts route work through the workspace instead of relying on a long one-off instruction. Replace bracketed text and attach or reference only material you are permitted to use.

## Start a session

> Read `WORKSPACE.md`, `context/current-priorities.md` and only the additional context required for this task. State what is known, assumed and missing. Use `workflows/README.md` to choose the one workflow that owns my primary decision: [decision]. Explain the routing in one sentence before starting.

## Discover opportunity

> Use `workflows/01-discover-opportunity.md` to compare [markets, segments or use cases]. Use the matching skills from `skills/README.md`. Keep source evidence, interpretation and assumptions separate, then recommend the next decision or validation step.

## Define strategy

> Use `workflows/02-define-strategy.md` to develop the PMM strategy for [product and audience]. Read the approved evidence and claims registers. Produce audience choices, positioning, message hierarchy, proof, trade-offs and open questions.

## Activate market

> Use `workflows/03-activate-market.md` for [launch or activation]. Confirm that the strategy and claims are approved before creating the plan. Define audience actions, channel and field deliverables, owners, dependencies, risks and success measures.

## Grow customers

> Use `workflows/04-grow-customers.md` to assess [adoption, retention, cross-sell or up-sell]. Apply eligibility and exclusion rules first. Separate aggregate patterns from account-level evidence and propose a validation step before commercial action.

## Learn and optimize

> Use `workflows/05-learn-optimize.md` to review [initiative or commercial outcomes]. Compare results with a defensible baseline, separate findings from explanations, and recommend what to continue, stop, change or test.

## Run a specialist analysis

> Use the `[skill-name]` skill. Before analysing, state its unit of analysis, scope, period, definitions, required fields and exclusions. Do not describe heuristics as predictions or observational patterns as causes.

## Review a draft

> Review this draft against `WORKSPACE.md`, the owning workflow and `context/claims-and-proof.md`. Identify unsupported claims, missing evidence, audience ambiguity, contradictions, unnecessary complexity and decisions that remain unresolved. Do not rewrite until the issues are visible.

## End a session

> Summarise what was completed, what was decided, what remains open and the next action. Propose updates to `context/decisions.md`, `context/current-priorities.md`, `context/evidence-register.md` and `context/session-log.md`. Show the proposed changes before editing durable context.
