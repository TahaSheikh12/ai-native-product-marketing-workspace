# PMM deliverable review

Use before approval or external distribution. Review the decision quality before polishing the format.

## Decision and audience

- Is the decision or intended behaviour explicit?
- Is the primary audience specific?
- Are poor-fit audiences or situations acknowledged?
- Does the format suit the decision maker?

## Evidence and claims

- Can material findings and claims be traced to evidence IDs?
- Are sample, period, population and comparator visible where relevant?
- Are facts, interpretations, recommendations and open questions separate?
- Are qualifiers preserved?
- Are contradictory or disconfirming findings represented?
- Are restricted, rejected or expired claims excluded?

## PMM quality

- Is the customer problem more prominent than the feature list?
- Is the true alternative, including status quo, represented?
- Is differentiation meaningful to the audience?
- Are trade-offs and constraints visible?
- Does every recommendation have an owner, next action or validation step?

## Analytical integrity

- Is the unit of analysis clear?
- Are denominators, exclusions and definitions explicit?
- Are counts shown alongside percentages where useful?
- Are heuristics labelled as heuristics?
- Are observational patterns kept distinct from causal conclusions?
- Are estimates kept distinct from forecasts and committed targets?
