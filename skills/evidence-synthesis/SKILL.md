# Evidence synthesis

Turn documents, interviews, surveys, notes and research extracts into decision-ready findings without collapsing fact, interpretation and speculation.

## MECE boundary

- **Unit of analysis:** a source, response or observation.
- **Owns:** source quality, thematic synthesis, contradictions and evidence traceability.
- **Does not own:** segment scoring, competitor comparisons, pricing models, revenue gaps, account scoring, win rates or program measurement.

## Establish the analysis

State the research question, decision, source population, collection period and known sampling limitations. Define what counts as direct evidence, inference and hypothesis.

## Analysis

1. Create a source register with provenance and date.
2. Assess relevance, credibility, recency and likely bias.
3. Extract observations before interpreting them.
4. Code themes consistently and retain contradictory evidence.
5. Report frequency only when the sampling method makes it meaningful.
6. Separate repeated observations from independent corroboration.
7. Translate findings into implications proportional to the evidence.
8. Route specialist questions to the relevant analytical skill.

## Output

- Question, decision and scope
- Source register and limitations
- Findings by theme with traceability
- Contradictions and disconfirming evidence
- Facts, interpretations and hypotheses
- PMM implications and recommended next evidence
