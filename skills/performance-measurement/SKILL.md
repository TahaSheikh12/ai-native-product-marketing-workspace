# Performance measurement

Determine whether a PMM initiative performed as intended and what should change, while separating activity, outcomes and causal inference.

## MECE boundary

- **Unit of analysis:** an initiative × audience or cohort × time period.
- **Owns:** measurement design, baselines, targets, funnel outcomes and program readouts.
- **Does not own:** individual opportunity diagnosis, general competitor profiling or causal claims unsupported by the design.

## Establish the analysis

State the initiative, audience, intended behaviour, measurement window, baseline, target and attribution limits. Define the unit of exposure and unit of outcome.

## Analysis

1. Translate the objective into a measurement chain: activity, reach, response, behaviour and business outcome.
2. Select a small set of leading and lagging indicators with clear definitions.
3. Establish data sources, owners, cadence and baseline before execution where possible.
4. Check exposure, eligibility, missingness and cohort comparability.
5. Compare results with target, baseline or a defensible comparison group.
6. Segment only when sample sizes and decision value justify it.
7. Separate observed change, plausible contribution and demonstrated causality.
8. Combine quantitative outcomes with qualitative evidence to explain results.
9. Recommend what to continue, stop, change or test next.

## Output

- Objective, audience and measurement design
- Metric definitions and data quality
- Results versus baseline, target or comparator
- Segment or cohort findings with counts
- Attribution limits and alternative explanations
- Decision and next test
- Owner and review date
