## Introduction

Explain that the purpose is to improve the buying and product experience, not to reopen the sale. Ask permission before recording or attributing comments.

## Decision context

1. What triggered the search or evaluation?
2. What would have happened if no change was made?
3. Who was involved and what did each person care about?
4. What alternatives were considered, including doing nothing?

## Decision process

5. Which criteria mattered most?
6. How did those criteria change during the process?
7. What information or experience increased confidence?
8. What created doubt, friction or delay?

## Outcome

9. What was the decisive factor?
10. Where did the selected option fit better?
11. Where did it fit worse or involve a compromise?
12. What could we have done differently?
