# Discover opportunity

Use this workflow to decide where a meaningful market or customer opportunity exists before choosing the strategy.

## Owns

An evidence-backed opportunity brief covering the problem, audience, market context, alternatives, potential value, uncertainty and next validation step.

## Does not own

Final positioning, launch execution, account-level expansion lists or retrospective performance decisions.

## Required inputs

- Decision the work must inform
- Existing customer, market and product evidence
- Relevant segments, use cases or geographies
- Known alternatives and constraints
- Time horizon and confidence required

## Supporting skills

Use only those needed:

- `evidence-synthesis` for mixed research sources
- `segment-opportunity-analysis` for prioritising segments or use cases
- `competitive-analysis` for alternative and market-structure evidence
- `revenue-whitespace-analysis` for internal mix or penetration gaps

## Process

1. Define the decision, scope and evidence standard.
2. Separate known facts, interpretations and hypotheses.
3. Assess customer need, market attractiveness, strategic fit and feasibility.
4. Compare plausible opportunities using consistent criteria.
5. Identify contradictions, risks and evidence gaps.
6. Recommend an opportunity, a validation plan or a decision to defer.

## Output

- Decision and scope
- Evidence base and limitations
- Customer problem and affected audience
- Opportunity options and comparison
- Recommended opportunity and rationale
- Risks, assumptions and disconfirming evidence
- Next validation action
