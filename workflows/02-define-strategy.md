# Define strategy

Use this workflow to make the core PMM choices after an opportunity has been identified.

## Owns

An approved PMM strategy brief: priority audience, market frame, positioning, message hierarchy, proof, offer implications and intended behaviour change.

## Does not own

Execution schedules, channel assets, seller training, customer expansion lists or post-program performance conclusions.

## Required inputs

- Approved opportunity or strategic problem
- Priority audience candidates
- Customer needs and decision criteria
- Product capabilities and constraints
- Alternatives and competitive evidence
- Proof and unresolved evidence gaps

## Supporting skills

- `evidence-synthesis` for research evidence
- `segment-opportunity-analysis` for target selection
- `competitive-analysis` for differentiation and market frame
- `pricing-packaging-analysis` when offer, package or value metric choices are in scope

## Process

1. Confirm the business objective and intended audience behaviour.
2. Choose the priority audience and explicitly deprioritise poor-fit groups.
3. Define the audience problem and true alternative, including status quo.
4. Express value as an outcome rather than a feature list.
5. Choose defensible differentiators and attach evidence.
6. Build the positioning statement and message hierarchy.
7. Record offer implications, trade-offs and evidence gaps.
8. Test for clarity, relevance, credibility and differentiation.

## Output

- Objective and intended behaviour change
- Priority and deprioritised audiences
- Market frame and alternatives
- Positioning statement
- Message hierarchy and proof
- Offer or packaging implications
- Objection themes and limitations
- Approval decisions and open questions
