# Activate market

Use this workflow to turn an approved PMM strategy into coordinated market, channel and field execution.

## Owns

A market activation plan covering launch, narrative rollout, content, channel briefs, sales enablement, owners, timing, dependencies and success signals.

## Does not own

Opportunity discovery, unresolved positioning choices, ongoing customer growth strategy or retrospective optimisation decisions.

## Required inputs

- Approved audience, positioning and message hierarchy
- Product or offer change being activated
- Desired audience action
- Launch window or campaign period
- Channels, field teams, constraints and owners
- Approved proof and claims

## Supporting skills

- `competitive-analysis` for battlecards and objection guidance
- `evidence-synthesis` when adapting approved customer evidence
- `performance-measurement` to define success measures and baselines

## Process

1. Confirm that the strategy is approved; route unresolved strategy choices to `02-define-strategy.md`.
2. Define the audience action and activation tier.
3. Build the narrative from problem to change to value to proof.
4. Map audience, channel and field deliverables without changing the underlying claim.
5. Create briefs for content, communications and seller conversations.
6. Assign owners, dependencies, reviews and deadlines.
7. Prepare objections, competitive guidance and poor-fit signals.
8. Define leading and lagging success measures.
9. Schedule readiness and post-activation reviews.

## Output

- Activation objective and audience action
- Narrative and invariant messages
- Launch and channel plan
- Content and communications briefs
- Sales enablement plan
- Owners, dependencies and timeline
- Risks and mitigations
- Success measures and review dates
