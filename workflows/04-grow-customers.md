# Grow customers

Use this workflow when the primary audience is an existing customer and the goal is adoption, retention, cross-sell or up-sell.

## Owns

A customer growth plan that links eligible customer opportunities to needs, value, timing, interventions and validation.

## Does not own

New-market selection, broad launch execution, raw account scoring without a customer strategy or final program performance conclusions.

## Required inputs

- Customer objective: adoption, retention, cross-sell or up-sell
- Customer segments and eligibility rules
- Product holdings, usage or adoption evidence
- Customer health, service and renewal context
- Offer, pricing and channel constraints

## Supporting skills

- `revenue-whitespace-analysis` for aggregate portfolio or penetration patterns
- `cross-sell-up-sell-analysis` for account-product opportunities
- `evidence-synthesis` for customer feedback and adoption barriers
- `performance-measurement` for intervention design and evaluation

## Process

1. Define the customer outcome and eligible population.
2. Diagnose the need, adoption barrier or expansion opportunity.
3. Separate aggregate patterns from account-level evidence.
4. Prioritise cohorts or accounts using transparent criteria.
5. Match interventions to customer need, readiness and timing.
6. Define discovery questions, channel actions and suppression rules.
7. Estimate value ranges without treating heuristics as forecasts.
8. Define a test, owner, success measure and review date.

## Output

- Objective and eligible customer population
- Evidence and opportunity diagnosis
- Prioritised cohorts or account opportunities
- Customer value narrative and recommended intervention
- Exclusions, risks and false-positive controls
- Owners, timing and next actions
- Measurement and validation plan
