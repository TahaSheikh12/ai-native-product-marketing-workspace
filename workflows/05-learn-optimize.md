# Learn and optimize

Use this workflow after market or commercial outcomes exist and the decision is what to continue, stop or change.

## Owns

A performance and learning review with traceable findings, explanations, decisions, owners and follow-up tests.

## Does not own

Initial opportunity selection, strategy creation, activation execution or speculative recommendations without outcome evidence.

## Required inputs

- Original objective, audience and intended behaviour
- Baseline, target and measurement period
- Activity, outcome and commercial data
- Customer, field or buyer feedback
- Known changes in cohort, market or execution

## Supporting skills

- `performance-measurement` for initiative, channel and cohort results
- `win-loss-analysis` for opportunity outcomes and buyer reasons
- `evidence-synthesis` for qualitative feedback
- Other skills only when the review raises a distinct analytical question

## Process

1. Restate the original decision, hypothesis and success measures.
2. Validate data coverage, comparability and timing.
3. Separate activity, leading indicators, outcomes and business impact.
4. Compare observed results with baseline or a defensible comparator.
5. Triangulate quantitative patterns with qualitative evidence.
6. Distinguish measured findings, plausible explanations and unknowns.
7. Decide what to continue, stop, change or test.
8. Assign owners, measures and review dates.

## Output

- Objective, scope and measurement design
- Results versus baseline or target
- Findings and limitations
- Explanations and competing hypotheses
- Decisions: continue, stop, change or test
- Owners, next actions and review date
- Durable learning to record in context
