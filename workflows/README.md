# Workflow router

The workflows form one PMM lifecycle. Choose the workflow whose **primary decision** matches the request.

| Stage | Workflow | Primary decision | Owned deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-discover-opportunity.md` | Where is the evidence-backed market or customer opportunity? | Opportunity brief |
| 2 | `02-define-strategy.md` | Who will we prioritise, what will we stand for and why will they believe us? | PMM strategy brief |
| 3 | `03-activate-market.md` | How will we take the strategy to market and equip each channel? | Market activation plan |
| 4 | `04-grow-customers.md` | How will we improve adoption, retention or expansion among customers? | Customer growth plan |
| 5 | `05-learn-optimize.md` | What happened, why, and what should change next? | Performance and learning review |

## Boundary rules

- Use **discover** before a target or opportunity has been chosen.
- Use **define** after evidence exists but audience, positioning, messaging or offer choices remain unresolved.
- Use **activate** after the strategy is approved and coordinated execution is required.
- Use **grow** when the primary population is existing customers.
- Use **learn** after observable outcomes exist and the decision is what to continue, stop or change.
- A request that spans stages creates separate deliverables. Do not merge the stages into an ambiguous catch-all plan.

Formats do not create new workflows. For example, a battlecard is usually an activation deliverable; a research deck is usually a discover deliverable; an executive readout is usually a learn deliverable.

Use `skills/README.md` to select supporting analytical methods.
